A FRAMEWORK FOR TRUST

Confidentiality and working agreements.

Governance, management situations and information about your teams require explicit boundaries, clarified when the engagement is scoped.

Protect session content

Individual discussions are not intended for disclosure to an employer or third party. Any information to be shared, its recipients and applicable limits to confidentiality are defined in the contractual framework.

Set reporting boundaries

For employer-funded coaching, reporting concerns the agreed objectives, progress and review. It does not reproduce personal disclosures from sessions. Reporting content is prepared with the participant within the agreed framework.

Share only what is necessary

For an initial enquiry, describe the issue without attaching sensitive documents or personal information about colleagues. Required materials, communication channels, access and retention arrangements are clarified before use.

Agree practical arrangements

Session frequency, availability between meetings, response times and rescheduling arrangements are agreed together. Remote meetings are organised so each participant can protect the confidentiality of the conversation.
