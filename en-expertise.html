EVALUATION AND OUTCOMES

Make progress observable.

Evaluation links coaching goals to professional practice. It distinguishes changes addressed in coaching from business results that also depend on external and organisational factors.

Establish a baseline

We identify challenging situations, observable behaviours and available indicators before taking action, giving the review a more reliable basis than general satisfaction alone.

Choose relevant indicators

Delegation: clear responsibilities and decisions at the appropriate level. Role transition: shared priorities and completed milestones. Cooperation: commitments fulfilled and agreed decision processes used.

Compare observations

The participant’s assessment is considered alongside completed actions and observable facts. Stakeholder feedback may contribute where relevant, within a scope and process agreed in advance.

Use reviews to guide decisions

Progress reviews help maintain, revise or refocus objectives. The final review makes progress, limitations and next steps explicit. Outcomes also depend on engagement and organisational conditions; no unsupported numerical gain is promised.
