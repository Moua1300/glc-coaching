EXECUTIVE AND MANAGEMENT COACHING

A rigorous framework for informed decisions.

Taking on a new role, developing leadership or leading change: coaching connects your responsibilities as a decision-maker with the realities of your organisation.

Understand the whole situation

We examine objectives, responsibilities, stakeholder relationships and operational constraints to distinguish personal leadership issues from team dynamics and organisational factors.

Agree clear objectives

Scope, priorities, expected changes and success criteria are defined at the outset. Objectives are realistic, observable and linked to specific professional situations.

Test progress in practice

Sessions prepare concrete actions: a difficult conversation, a delegation agreement, a decision or a management routine. Experience between sessions informs the next stage of work.

Create a confidential space

Confidentiality and any reporting to the sponsor are agreed at the outset, providing space for candid reflection that is distinct from a performance appraisal.
