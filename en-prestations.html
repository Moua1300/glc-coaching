OUR METHODOLOGY

From a clear brief to lasting autonomy.

The pace and duration reflect the challenge, the level of responsibility and the conditions for implementation. Five stages provide a framework adapted to each engagement.

01 — Clarify the request

An initial discussion distinguishes the stated request, the professional issues and the intended outcomes. We identify stakeholders, constraints, resources and conditions for success before proposing an approach.

02 — Agree the framework

Objectives, scope, schedule, roles, reviews and confidentiality are documented. For employer-funded coaching, a three-party discussion brings the participant, sponsor and coach together to agree expectations and reporting boundaries.

03 — Analyse real situations

Sessions explore actual decisions, working relationships, role transitions and change. Questions, reflective dialogue and interaction analysis help distinguish facts, assumptions and available options.

04 — Experiment and adjust

An action plan specifies commitments, deadlines and observable criteria. The manager tests practices between sessions; a structured review identifies effects, obstacles and adjustments.

05 — Evaluate and consolidate

A final review compares the starting point with observed changes, identifies learning and remaining priorities, and establishes a consolidation plan to support independent progress.

Practical working materials

Depending on the brief: an agreed scope, stakeholder map, delegation matrix, conversation preparation, action plan and progress tracker. Deliverables and recipients are agreed upfront; personal session notes are not a report to the sponsor.
