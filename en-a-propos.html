EXPERIENCE AND PROFESSIONAL STANCE

Practical experience in support of your responsibilities.

Grégoire Lefebvre brings more than 28 years of experience in recruitment, management, consulting and training, including seven years leading a training organisation.

Understand the decision-maker’s constraints

Entrepreneurial experience informs work on trade-offs between quality, time, resources and team engagement. Coaching takes account of the leader’s actual authority and responsibilities.

Distinguish coaching, consulting and training

Coaching develops your thinking and choices. Consulting provides a reasoned recommendation. Training builds skills through practice. The intervention mode is made explicit to keep the working relationship clear.

Challenge constructively

Attentive listening is paired with rigorous questioning: testing an assumption, identifying a blind spot or examining consequences. The coach supports your judgement; responsibility for decisions remains yours.

Transfer learning into practice

Instructional design experience and support for more than 2,800 people across the professional journey underpin an approach based on real work, experimentation, reflection and sustainable practice.
